What I work on and how I approach it.

I like to understand a system from the model outward: how it works, what software it needs, how it behaves under measurement, and whether it is useful in practice.

That approach has taken me from a VR-to-ROS humanoid simulation pipeline to VGG pruning and quantization experiments and diffusion-based metamaterial design.

At BMW Group, I identify and assess AI opportunities in operational workflows. Through TUM.ai, I support international partnerships and student makeathons.
